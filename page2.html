Turkey Club Sandwich
  • Italian
  • Turkey Club
  • Philly Cheesesteak

Ingredients

  • Applewood smoked bacon
  • Hearty white sandwich bread
  • Vine-ripened tomatoes
  • Kosher salt
  • Ground black pepper
  • Mayonnaise
  • Romaine lettuce leaves
  • Deli turkey

Instructions

  1. Cook bacon in oven for 20 minutes

  2. Toast bread in oven till golden brown

  3. Put salt and pepper on tomato

  4. Spread mayo on bread and add bacon, lettuce, and tomato

  5. Add the turkey to the sandwich

  6. Cut the sandwich in half and dig in!
