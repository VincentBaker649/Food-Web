Philly Cheesesteak
  • Italian
  • Turkey Club
  • Philly Cheesesteak

Ingredients

  • Steak
  • Onions
  • Peppers
  • Hoagie Roll
  • Cheeze Whiz

Instructions

  1. Thinly slice your steak

  2. Throw steak in pan with onions and peppers

  3. Toast hoagie in oven

  4. Add steak and onion/pepper mix to your hoagie

  5. Add Cheeze Whiz

  6. Cut the sandwich in half and dig in!
